CBBD League Rulebook

CBBD 2-Day Basketball League: Official Rules & Regulations

1. League Eligibility & Membership

  • Exclusive Access: The league is strictly open to CBBD Members only.
  • Eligible Statuses: Any Active, Inactive, Veteran, and Alumni members are welcome to join and participate. Teams must verify membership status prior to the tournament start.

2. Tournament Format & Game Duration

  • Format: The tournament will follow a Double-Elimination format (Teams must lose twice to be officially eliminated).
  • Day 1: All 4 teams play their opening match. Winners play in a non-elimination clash. No team is sent home on Day 1.
  • Day 2: Bracket resolves into Semis and the single-game Championship.
  • Game Duration: Each game consists of four (4) quarters of 10 minutes each.
  • Warm-up: A 10-minute warm-up period will be given for each team and each game prior to tip-off.

3. Timeouts

  • Regulation Time: Each team is granted two (2) full timeouts and two (2) 30-second timeouts per half.
  • Carry-overs: Timeouts do not carry over from the first half to the second half, or from regulation to overtime.
  • Overtime: In case of an overtime period, each team will be given an additional one (1) timeout.
  • Sudden Death (3rd Overtime): If the game is still tied after the second overtime (2OT), the third overtime (3OT) is played as Sudden Death: the first team to score a basket wins the game.

4. Player Participation & Playing Time Rule

  • Mandatory Playing Time: All players listed on each team's official roster must log at least 2 minutes of playing time per game.

Consequence for Non-Compliance:

  • First Offense / Warning: If a team fails to give a roster player their mandatory 2 minutes, the offending team will receive a formal warning and must substitute the player in immediately.
  • Forfeiture Risk: Persistent or intentional refusal to play registered roster players may result in a forfeiture of the game or disciplinary action determined by the league commissioner. Team captains are responsible for tracking rotations.

5. Code of Conduct, Sportsmanship & Disciplinary Rules

  • Zero Tolerance for Misbehavior: Any kind of bad, unsportsmanlike, or unpleasant behavior is strictly prohibited. Violence, fighting, or physical threats will result in an immediate lifetime ban from CBBD sports events.
  • Technical Fouls for Behavior: Any player or coach displaying disrespectful behavior, excessive complaining, or minor unsportsmanlike conduct will be assessed an automatic Technical Foul.
  • Disqualification (Ejection): Receiving two (2) technical fouls in a single game will automatically result in the player's immediate disqualification (ejection) from that game, plus an automatic suspension for the team's next game.

6. Officiating & Protests

  • Finality of Calls: The referee's call is final. Judgement calls (e.g., fouls, traveling, out-of-bounds) cannot be protested or overturned.
  • Captain's Dialogue: Only the designated Team Captain may calmly address the referees or table officials to clarify a rule interpretation during a dead ball.

7. Additional League Guidelines

  • Uniforms & Gear: Teams should wear matching jerseys or shirts with visible numbers. Proper basketball shoes are required; playing in slippers, casual sneakers, or barefoot is strictly prohibited for safety reasons.
  • Forfeit Time: Teams must be ready to play at their scheduled game time. A 10-minute grace period will be given. If a team fails to field at least 5 players after the grace period, they will forfeit the match.

Tie-Breakers (if applicable in group stages):

  1. Head-to-head record
  2. Point differential (plus/minus)
  3. Total points scored
  • Injuries & Substitutions: If a player is injured and unable to complete their mandatory 2-minute playing time, an exception is made, and an immediate substitution is allowed without penalty.
